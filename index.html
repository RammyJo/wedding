Frequently Asked Questions

A few answers to help you plan for the day.

✦
What time should I arrive?

Please arrive around 3:30 PM and be seated before the ceremony begins at 4:00 PM.

Is parking available?

Parking is available at or near the venue, but spaces may be limited. Please follow the venue team's directions when you arrive and allow extra time.

When should I RSVP?

Please respond by November 10, 2026. This helps us prepare your seat and meal. Use the RSVP section on this page and follow the instructions shown after preparing your response.

Can I bring a plus-one?

We have a limited guest list, so we're only able to accommodate the people specifically named on each invitation. Thank you for understanding.

Do you have a gift preference?

Your presence is already a gift to us. Monetary gifts are welcome but absolutely not expected. If you would like to give one, the payment QR codes will be added in the gift section once confirmed.

May I take photos or videos?

We'd love for you to capture happy memories. During the ceremony, please keep phones and cameras away from the aisle so our official photographer and videographer can work without obstruction.
